Our Mission

Every veteran who builds a business deserves a community rallying behind them. VetConnect bridges the gap between brave entrepreneurs and the neighbors who are ready to support them.

From a neighborhood bakery to a skilled trades company, veteran-owned businesses enrich communities. VetConnect makes them impossible to miss—and easy to champion.

Meet the Founder

Suri Charlu

Student Entrepreneur & Developer

Suri Charlu is a high school student entrepreneur driven by a passion for technology and community impact. Recognizing the challenge veterans face when launching businesses, Suri built Veteran-Connect to give these heroes the visibility and support they deserve. Through code, creativity, and purpose, Suri is proving that the next generation of innovators is already here—building solutions that matter.
